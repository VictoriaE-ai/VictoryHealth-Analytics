## Featured Project: MedSafe-R

MedSafe-R explores a practical question:

**Which medical devices need engineering attention, why, and what could this mean for patient safety?**

The current corrected **v1.3 synthetic dataset** includes:

- 1,200 medical devices.
- 4,366 retained maintenance events.
- 7,193 retained monthly operational records.
- 223 retained safety events affecting 206 distinct devices.
- 18 fictional regulatory signals.
- 1,200 records prepared for machine-learning development.

Completed analysis identifies 253 overdue devices (21.1%) under documented PM-date assumptions and evaluates logistic regression and random forest baselines against an engineering heuristic. The report includes uncertainty estimates and reproducible code.

[Explore the project and chart report](https://github.com/VictoriaE-ai/MedSafe-R#start-here). Next stages are to update Power BI, add model explanations and calibration checks, and seek clinical-engineering feedback and external validation.

## Responsible Use

MedSafe-R Dataset v1.3 is entirely synthetic and contains no real patient or hospital data. Its regulatory signals are fictional.

Portfolio prototypes demonstrate learning and development. They are not validated tools for clinical or maintenance decisions. Completed work, ongoing development and planned features are labelled separately.
