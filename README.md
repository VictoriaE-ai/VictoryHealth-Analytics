## Featured Project: MedSafe-R

MedSafe-R explores a practical question:

**Which medical devices need engineering attention, why, and what could this mean for patient safety?**

The completed synthetic dataset includes:

- 1,200 medical devices.
- 4,751 maintenance events.
- 7,200 monthly operational records.
- 238 safety events.
- 18 fictional regulatory signals.
- 1,200 records prepared for machine-learning development.

The next stages are to complete the Power BI dashboard, document maintenance-priority scoring, and evaluate a predictive-maintenance model.

## Methods and Tools

Project work is being organised around Power BI dashboards, Python and SQL analysis, reliability indicators, risk prioritisation and explainable machine learning. Each project will document the tools actually used, methods, results and limitations.

## Responsible Use

MedSafe-R Dataset v1 is entirely synthetic and contains no real patient or hospital data. Its regulatory signals are fictional.

Portfolio prototypes demonstrate learning and development. They are not validated tools for clinical or maintenance decisions. Completed work, ongoing development and planned features are labelled separately.
