# VictoryHealth Analytics — Five Future Project Ideas

**Recorded:** 26 October 2026  
**Status:** Idea backlog / proposed concepts, not completed or validated healthcare products.

## Purpose
Maintain five possible future projects aligned with the VictoryHealth Analytics vision: *Engineering safer, smarter, and more connected healthcare through data and responsible AI.*

These are proposals for exploration. Rankings reflect an initial assessment of brand fit and feasible prototype scope, not a validated research-gap or market analysis. Existing related work: [MedSafe-R](https://github.com/VictoriaE-ai/MedSafe-R) and [VHA-AIRAF](https://github.com/VictoriaE-ai/VHA-AIRAF).

| Initial priority | Idea | Core objective | Suggested first deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **VHA-MultiData Ready** | Assess how prepared a health or social care organisation is to safely integrate clinical, device, genomic and social determinants of health (SDOH) data. | Literature/gap comparison matrix and a scoped assessment specification; later a Python/Streamlit demonstration. |
| 2 | **MedSafe-R Data Fusion** | Explore whether combining maintenance records, device operating conditions and synthetic safety-event information helps prioritise engineering attention. | A reproducible synthetic-data integration experiment, building on MedSafe-R. |
| 3 | **VHA-SocialCare Insights** | Explore links among population-level deprivation measures, service access and healthcare outcomes without making individual clinical decisions. | A documented exploratory dashboard using open, aggregate or synthetic data. |
| 4 | **VHA-Genomic Data Readiness** | Test genomic data quality, metadata, provenance, interoperability and governance readiness, without individual genetic interpretation. | Synthetic metadata validation checks and a requirements checklist. |
| 5 | **VHA-HealthData Trust** | Compare tamper-evident data-access auditing, role-based permissions and consent-status recordkeeping against conventional approaches; evaluate whether blockchain adds benefit. | An audit-trail prototype and comparative design evaluation. |

## Shared design principles
- Patient safety, meaningful human oversight and responsible AI.
- Use synthetic or appropriately aggregated public data in initial open-source prototypes; do not publish identifiable patient data.
- Document methods, data provenance, risks, limitations, and status (idea / prototype / tested / externally validated).
- Review UK health data and information-governance requirements before any real-world usage.
- Never describe a demonstration score or dashboard as clinical approval or proof of compliance.

## Recommended sequence
1. **Start with VHA-MultiData Ready:** review existing NHS and UK healthcare readiness tools and verify whether a specific gap exists.
2. Define potential users, scope, core questions, and a transparent scoring and evidence model.
3. Build a small demonstration only after the problem and design are adequately defined.
4. Continue **MedSafe-R Data Fusion** as a logical extension of existing work.
5. Reassess priorities based on feedback, data availability and technical capacity.

## First proposed research question
> How can a transparent, evidence-informed assessment tool evaluate the organisational readiness of UK health and social care providers to integrate emerging technologies and multimodal healthcare data safely, effectively and equitably?
